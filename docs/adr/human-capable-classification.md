# Human-capable and corroborated-indicator POC policy

Policy revision recorded on 30 October 2026 following the owner's explicit instructions in this chat: a browser or normal email client may be controlled by either a person or an agent and still receive **Human-capable**. This replaces the active release's Human label and its inferred-manual-controller meaning. The green preset remains preset4. No existing Outlook category has been renamed or provisioned; only local contracts and dry-run previews change here.

Human-capable means the complete configured message inspection is compatible with a normal client and reveals no MCP/API/known-automation indicator or conflicting evidence. It does not establish who clicked Send, exclude browser-driven agents or measure biological-human identity. A normal-client-compatible message can therefore be Human-capable whether its body was typed, pasted from an assistant or supplied by an agent. Incomplete inspection, known automation, source errors and conflicts remain Unknown. Known automation does not automatically imply AI.

The owner also selected the [corroborated-indicator AI proposal](corroborated-agent-poc-rule.md). The enrolled Claude/own-mailbox route may produce AI Agent from the known MCP declaration plus authenticated successful Send evidence, current publisher pin, scope/Message-ID/time consistency and a unique provider-observed Sent/received-copy pair. The rule is deliberately narrow. It does not assert independent forensic identity between Microsoft's audit store IDs and Graph received IDs. Observed collisions, conflicts, failed or incomplete audit searches and revoked/mismatching publishers prevent an AI label. An unobservable full replacement preserving all correlation fields remains an explicitly accepted POC risk.

The [current machine-readable policy](../../config/outlook-detection-policy.json), runtime schemas and implementation plan govern this release. The Phase 0 frozen baseline and historical evidence reports remain unchanged as records of their earlier acceptance. Their Human naming and independent-receipt-proof requirement are superseded only for this explicitly revised POC policy. The old binary terminal demo is also preserved separately.

This is semantic qualification of approved rules. Independent false-positive/recall measurements remain Phase 4 work; no 0.1% accuracy claim, universal agent coverage, immediate audit-delivery guarantee or production-regulatory certification follows from authored tests or a few live cases. Detection remains the sole product; category write permissions/implementation and actual Outlook rendering remain later phases.
